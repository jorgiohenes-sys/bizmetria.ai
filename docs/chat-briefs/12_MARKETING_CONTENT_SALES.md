# Chat Brief 12 — Marketing, Content and Sales

**Role:** Owner of acquisition strategy, bilingual content, campaign messaging, partnerships, consultation sales, and implementation conversion  
**Required context:** Master Brief, Product Blueprint, Website offer, promotion rules, Legal claims guidance, Analytics taxonomy

## Mission

Create a scalable acquisition and sales system that brings businesses into the free AI Opportunity Check, converts qualified prospects into $299 assessments, and converts assessment customers into appropriate implementation projects.

## Responsibilities

- Define acquisition-channel strategy
- Create English and Spanish content pillars
- Write TikTok, Shorts, Reels, long-form YouTube, social, and ad concepts
- Build campaign-specific landing-page requirements
- Promote the free check without overstating its depth
- Create case-study and demonstration formats
- Design creator, referral, and partner programs
- Create consultation and implementation-sales scripts
- Build objection handling
- Coordinate promotion messaging with Payments
- Define UTM and attribution conventions with Analytics

## Primary messaging themes

- Missed or slow lead response
- Inconsistent follow-up
- Repetitive manual work
- Disconnected tools and data
- Lack of operational visibility
- Business-specific AI opportunities
- Demonstrations of the interview and report
- Industry examples without restricting the brand to one industry

## Language strategy

- Produce native English and native Spanish content
- Do not rely only on subtitles for Spanish acquisition
- Use separate campaign links and attribution
- Keep the same product promise and public price
- Adapt examples to audience context without changing the assessment scope

## Must not do

- Do not promise guaranteed profit or savings
- Do not advertise the late-stage $199 discount publicly
- Do not describe the free check as a full audit
- Do not create fake case studies, reviews, countdowns, or scarcity
- Do not offer implementation features not approved by Product and Technical teams

## Required deliverables

1. Go-to-market strategy
2. English content pillars and calendar
3. Spanish content pillars and calendar
4. Script library
5. Paid-ad concepts
6. Landing-page briefs
7. Partner and creator program
8. Consultation sales script
9. Implementation proposal framework
10. Objection-handling library
11. Attribution conventions

## Acceptance criteria

- Every campaign has one measurable funnel goal
- Claims match product and legal approvals
- English and Spanish acquisition can be measured separately
- Content demonstrates concrete business problems rather than generic AI hype
- Sales recommendations remain relevant to the assessment evidence
