# Chat Brief 03 — Brand, Website and UX

**Role:** Customer-facing brand, information architecture, copy, and interaction owner  
**Required context:** Master Brief, Product Blueprint, Free Audit contracts, Legal requirements

## Mission

Design a credible, conversion-focused, bilingual website and customer experience that moves visitors from discovery to free check, paid checkout, voice interview, report delivery, and consultation.

## Responsibilities

- Define brand expression and interface principles
- Create the sitemap and page hierarchy
- Design English and Spanish user journeys
- Design the free-check question experience and result page
- Design the $299 offer page and Stripe handoff
- Design paid onboarding and language-specific phone instructions
- Design report status and delivery experience
- Prepare production-ready copy in both languages
- Define responsive behavior and accessibility requirements
- Define analytics events with QA/Analytics

## Required pages and states

- Home EN and ES
- Free AI Opportunity Check
- Free result page with limited insights and locked previews
- Paid assessment offer
- Stripe return/success and cancellation states
- Expanded questionnaire
- English phone-instructions page
- Spanish phone-instructions page
- Interview completed state
- Report processing and report-ready states
- Consultation booking
- FAQ, About, Contact
- Privacy, Terms, recording consent, refund policy

## Must not do

- Do not invent scoring logic
- Do not create claims not approved by Product or Legal
- Do not show the English number on the Spanish paid path or vice versa
- Do not expose detailed paid recommendations in the free result
- Do not create different product promises by language

## Required deliverables

1. Sitemap
2. End-to-end UX flow
3. Wireframes for primary mobile and desktop states
4. English website copy
5. Spanish adapted copy
6. Component and design-system specification
7. Error, empty, and recovery states
8. Analytics-event map
9. Frontend acceptance criteria

## Acceptance criteria

- The value proposition is understandable without technical knowledge
- The free check can be completed in approximately 3–5 minutes
- The $299 offer is clear and credible
- Language choice persists through the customer journey
- All critical actions work on mobile
- Legal consent and recording disclosure are visible at the correct moments
